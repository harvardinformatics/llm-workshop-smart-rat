# My LLM Workshop Journal

This is your running log of interactions with your LLM during the workshop. The goal is to slow down and reflect — not just generate outputs, but understand what you're doing and why.

**How to use this:** After each working session (or every 30–45 minutes), write a short entry. Don't edit previous entries — just add new ones at the bottom. You'll look back at this at the end of the workshop.
